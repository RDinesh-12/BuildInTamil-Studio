Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 | Effective Date: November 25, 2026
BuildInTamil Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Astral Terraces and related interactive games. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you access our games across web browsers, YouTube Playables, and Facebook Instant Games.

1. Data Collection & Minimization

We respect your privacy and follow strict data minimization principles. We do not require account registration, nor do we collect sensitive personal identification:

  • Gameplay and Progress Data: High scores, level progress (worlds 1–10), collected star relics, sound settings, and control preferences are saved locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage.
  • Platform User Identifiers: When played on platforms like Facebook Instant Games or YouTube Playables, the platform provides anonymous player IDs or nicknames strictly to populate in-game leaderboards or trigger platform cloud saves.
  • Technical Telemetry: Purely local client-side viewport metrics (device orientation, screen width/height, touch capabilities) used dynamically to scale camera angles and controls.

2. Use of Information

Any data processed by our games is used exclusively to:

  • Save your puzzle solving progress and levels unlocked.
  • Synchronize audio volume and mute state with host platforms.
  • Display session high scores and completion times.

3. Third-Party Platform Partners

When playing Astral Terraces via partner platforms, their respective privacy policies also apply:

  • Meta / Facebook Gaming: Operates under Meta's Privacy Policy. Instant Games SDK interacts with Facebook cloud storage to persist your game score.
  • Google / YouTube Playables: Operates under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

You have full control over your saved data. You can delete or reset all game progress at any time by clicking "Reset Progress" within the in-game Settings menu or by clearing your browser cache.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are suitable for all age groups. We do not intentionally collect or maintain personal information from children under 13.
